02-vp-monogram
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original software logo/icon concept exploration for Virtual Persona Core, a platform for persistent living AI characters, memories and desktop companions. The previous circular human-profile design was rejected. Start fresh. Create a polished square graphic logo presentation: a single large original emblem centered in the upper two thirds, with exact small wordmark "Virtual Persona" and tiny spaced "CORE" below. One small inverse-color sample of that same emblem in the bottom right, no other decoration. Strong simple silhouette, refined optical balance, clean flat vector-like edges, reads at favicon size. No human head, no profile, no brain, no enclosing ring, no power symbol, no circuitry, no generic AI sparkle, no gradients, no glow, no bevel, no texture, no photographed objects, no watermarks.
Direction 02: a confident custom VP monogram. Invent one compact interlocking glyph combining the sharp descending stroke of V and a restrained curved upper bowl of P into a single architectural symbol. Bold strokes, deliberate negative-space cuts, beautifully balanced bespoke typography. The mark should feel like a distinctive independent developer tool, abstract first and legible as VP on second look. Avoid resembling any existing famous technology logo. Ink black on warm off-white. Small bottom-left label exactly "02 / MONOGRAM".
