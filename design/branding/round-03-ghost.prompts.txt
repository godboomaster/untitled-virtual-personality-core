05-ghost-fold
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: logo/icon concept for Virtual Persona Core, a platform for living AI characters that remember their user, live between messages and appear as desktop companions.
User direction: explore a GHOST theme, combining the confidence of a bold geometric software monogram with the personality of a tiny friendly spirit.
Create a square polished flat graphic concept presentation. ONE large emblem in the upper two thirds, exact clean wordmark "Virtual Persona" below with small spaced "CORE", one miniature application icon of the same mark at bottom right. Elegant generous whitespace and strong composition.
The ghost must have a unique, bold, extremely simple outer contour, exactly two eye cutouts, no mouth, no accessories. Friendly but quietly mysterious, mature rather than babyish. The icon must read at tiny sizes. Precision geometry and deliberate negative space. Avoid generic Halloween sheet ghosts, Pac-Man ghosts, Snapchat resemblance, robot heads, chat bubbles, enclosing circular rings, human profiles, brains, sparkles, horns, fangs, illustration detail, extra characters. No gradients, glow, texture, shadows, bevels, 3D, stock mockup objects or watermark. Crisp flat vector-like artwork.
Direction: FOLDED GHOST. Design an angular ghost with a broad softly rounded dome and an asymmetrical lower edge made of two decisive diagonal folds. A central angular negative-space notch should subtly suggest a V in the floating hem, never literal text inside the ghost. Compact almost-square silhouette with curved shoulders and sharp folded lower tips. Two small calm vertical eye cutouts spaced with personality. This is a clever memorable geometric emblem first, with a ghost revealed immediately after. Solid ivory mark on graphite #161918, black eye holes. Tiny bottom-left label exactly "01 / FOLD".
