15-vpc-ghost-ligature
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original monogram/ghost logo for the software project Virtual Persona Core.
Input images 1 and 2 are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: the user likes their bold black ghost, two eye cutouts, rounded head and asymmetrical angular lower edge. You may redesign the geometry substantially.
User request: COMBINE ALL THREE LATIN INITIALS V, P, C AND THE GHOST INTO ONE UNIFIED EMBLEM. Both the letters and ghost should be discoverable; do not merely hide an illegible P in a small notch. Previous attempts failed in opposite ways: a big literal P cut into the ghost's belly looked like a mouth, while a tiny rounded notch didn't read as a letter.
Design with the sophistication of a custom typographic ligature: shared strokes, deliberate counterforms, strong silhouette. Use the ACTUAL structures of uppercase V, P, C with enough familiar geometry to identify all three. The ghost grows out of the monogram's geometry and exactly two calm eye cutouts. Letters must constitute the emblem itself, not be printed on a ghost or added underneath. One unified symbol, not a ghost beside the text VPC, not three ghosts, not a separate mascot plus badge.
Presentation: a square flat warm-ivory #F4F0E7 concept card. Large black #151817 emblem occupying upper 65%, exact clean sans-serif wordmark "Virtual Persona" below with small spaced "CORE". Style: bold, compact, refined, playful but mature. Clean flat vector-like edges, broad strokes, readable negative spaces, rounded upper geometry with a restrained angular lower silhouette. Two small eye cutouts only; no mouth. No gradients, shadows, textures, grain, lighting, 3D, neon, decorative stars, robot parts, human heads, watermark or extra slogans.
Direction 11 / LIGATURE:
Start from tightly interlocked uppercase "VPC" as a compact custom ligature, roughly 1.25 times wider than tall. Preserve a legible V on the left through two strong diagonals and an acute lower point. P is the upright central structure with an unmistakable rounded upper bowl. C is the broad right-hand curved stroke with a generous opening facing RIGHT. Share a stroke between V/P and share part of the bowl between P/C; avoid redundant parallel outlines. Integrate a single soft ghost-head arch into the top of the central P structure, and add two small upright eye slots there. The lower V-shaped point and a shorter opposite tail make the ligature read as a friendly floating ghost. Monogram first, ghost second. Prefer legible VPC and clever connected typography over forcing the original solid ghost outline. Bottom-left label exactly "11 / LIGATURE".
